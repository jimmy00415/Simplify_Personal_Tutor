## Description

Hong Kong Buddy helps HKBU students get verified campus next steps and practise useful everyday Cantonese. It is an AI assistant, not a student, staff member, or official university representative.

What you can do without an account:

- Ask the campus AI senior about registration, halls, food, and student services. Campus facts include reviewed sources and a verification date.
- Practise Teaching or Free Talk scenarios. This is conversation practice, not pronunciation assessment.
- Translate a supervised elderly or community visit in four directions. Ask staff to confirm important meaning.
- Keep a small offline phrasebook and a Today habit card saved on this iPhone only.

Voice messages are optional. A recording is uploaded for transcription only after you agree, and the transcript stays editable until you tap Send. Generated voice never autoplays.

Privacy: typed text is stored in Hong Kong (`asia-east2`) and sent to Google Vertex AI on the global Gemini endpoint. Speech uses Google Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech in `asia-southeast1`. See the in-app privacy notice.
