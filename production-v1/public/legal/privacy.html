Hong Kong Buddy

Privacy and data use

Notice version: 2026-09-10-ios-combined. This app is an AI assistant for HKBU students. It is not a student, staff member, or official university representative.

What we process

A guest session cookie identifies this device’s conversation. Typed text is stored in Cloud SQL in asia-east2 and is sent to Google Vertex AI on the global Gemini endpoint to produce a reply.

Optional voice uploads a recording to Google Speech-to-Text in asia-southeast1 only after you grant microphone consent. You can edit the transcript. It is not sent as a chat message until you tap Send. Optional generated voice uses Google Text-to-Speech in the same Singapore region.

Campus answers may include reviewed HKBU page citations. Practice and visit translation do not attach the campus knowledge corpus.

What we cannot promise

Google may process prompts, transcripts, and synthesis requests in the regions above, including the global Gemini endpoint. We do not claim that data stays only in one city, and we do not claim that providers delete every copy immediately. Retention follows this product’s session-deletion and media-cleanup jobs plus each provider’s own terms.

Your choices

Use Clear conversation to revoke this guest session and queue stored media for deletion. History does not follow you to another browser or device. For access or correction requests, use the support page.

我們處理什麼

訪客工作階段 cookie 用來識別此裝置上的對話。你輸入的文字會存放於 asia-east2 的 Cloud SQL，並送到 Google Vertex AI 的 global Gemini 端點以產生回覆。

可選語音會在你同意使用麥克風後，把錄音上傳到 asia-southeast1 的 Google Speech-to-Text。你可以先改逐字稿，按傳送後才會成為對話。可選的助手語音使用同一新加坡區域的 Google Text-to-Speech。

我們不能保證的事

Google 可能在上述區域（包括全球 Gemini 端點）處理提示、逐字稿與語音合成。我們不會聲稱資料只留在單一城市，也不會聲稱供應商會即時刪除所有副本。

使用「清除對話」可撤銷此訪客工作階段並排隊刪除已存媒體。如需查閱或更正，請到支援頁。
