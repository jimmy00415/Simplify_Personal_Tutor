Hong Kong Buddy

Support, access, and correction

This product is an unofficial AI assistant for HKBU students. For App Store review notes, privacy questions, or a request to access or correct guest-session data we still hold, contact the operator.

Email: support@hongkongbuddy.app

Include the approximate time you used the app and whether the request is about campus chat, practice, visit translation, or a voice draft. Do not send passwords, student-card numbers, or recordings in email.

You can also use Clear conversation in the app to revoke the current guest session.

支援、查閱與更正

如需查閱或更正我們仍持有的訪客工作階段資料，請電郵 support@hongkongbuddy.app。

請說明大約使用時間，以及問題屬校園對話、廣東話練習、探訪翻譯或語音草稿。請勿在電郵中傳送密碼、學生證號碼或錄音。
